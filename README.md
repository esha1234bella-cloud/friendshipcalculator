# 💖 Amistad — Friendship Calculator & Celebration Portal

A single-page web experience celebrating the beauty of genuine companionship, featuring an interactive Friendship Calculator, cute purple-to-pink gradient styling, minimal floating background animations, and curated authentic photography.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎀 **Top Marquee Ribbon**: Continuous scrolling ribbon celebrating friendship.
- 🔮 **Interactive Friendship Calculator**:
  - Enter two friends' names & select a friendship vibe.
  - Generates deterministic compatibility %, SVG radial meter, friendship archetype, metric breakdowns (*Trust, Laughter, Telepathy, Adventure*), and confetti celebration.
  - **Copy Bestie Certificate**: One-click copyable certificate to send to your friends.
- 🎨 **Cute Aesthetic & Purple/Pink Color Grading**: Glassmorphism cards with subtle glow borders and Google Fonts (*Fredoka*, *Quicksand*, *Pacifico*).
- 🎈 **Minimal Floating Background**: Soft, lightweight floating pinkish & reddish hearts and balloons.
- 📸 **Importance of Friendship & Polaroid Gallery**: Curated Unsplash photography with tilted polaroid frames and washi tape aesthetics.
- 🥠 **Interactive Bestie Hub**:
  - **Friendship Fortune Cookie**: Crack open sweet affirmations.
  - **Virtual Friendship Sticky Wall**: Pin custom pastel notes to your besties.
  - **Friendship Bucket List**: Interactive checklist.

## 🚀 Live Demo / Local Usage

```bash

cd friendshipcalculator
# Open index.html in your browser
```
